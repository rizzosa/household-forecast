Model assumptions

Balances use nominal, pre-tax dollars and fixed returns. Year 0 is today. Annual rows apply growth and monthly savings, then withdrawals, then any scheduled purchase. Extra move-in savings start in the month after you move and stop at closing. In manual mode, enter savings after regular expenses, including any new mortgage. In budget mode, the calculator holds pay and expenses steady and subtracts the new mortgage and PMI from take-home pay after closing.

Existing home equity stays flat until sale. Sale proceeds go toward closing first, with any surplus added to household savings. Other withdrawals use household savings first, then both taxable investment balances in proportion to their size, then the trust if enabled. Retirement balances stay invested. Include property taxes, insurance, and maintenance in your budget inputs. The forecast excludes taxes on investment sales and trust distribution restrictions.
